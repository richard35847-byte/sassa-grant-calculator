SASSA June 2026 Payment Dates Confirmed — All Grants

SASSA has confirmed the full payment schedule for June 2026. Older Persons and War Veterans will be paid on Monday 1 June, Disability and Care Dependency on 2 June, and Children's grants on 3 June. The SRD R370 payment window runs from approximately 24 to 30 June.

SRD R370 Verification Delays April 2026 — SASSA Response

SASSA responded to reports of verification delays affecting the R370 SRD grant. SASSA Grant Amounts 2026 — Annual Adjustment Confirmed

Following the national budget, SASSA confirmed adjusted grant amounts for 2026. The Older Persons, Disability, and Care Dependency grants were increased to R2,400 per month. Child Support Grant increased to R580 per child. The SRD R370 amount remains R370 pending further policy review.

ITSAA Processing Times Update — Appeal Backlog Addressed

The Independent Tribunal for Social Assistance Appeals (ITSAA) acknowledged a backlog in SRD appeal processing. Officials confirmed that appeals submitted before February 2026 should receive decisions by end of April 2026. Applicants were urged to keep their contact details updated to receive SMS notifications.

SASSA Urges Beneficiaries to Fix Identity Verification Failures

SASSA issued guidance for beneficiaries with "Identity Verification Failed" statuses. Officials stated that many failures result from outdated Home Affairs records and encouraged affected applicants to use the online verification portal or visit their nearest SASSA office.
