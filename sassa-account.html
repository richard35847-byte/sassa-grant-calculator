SASSA Account Help 2026

Step-by-step guides for banking details, phone number updates, applying, and fixing account issues.

This page covers the most common SASSA account tasks for the SRD R370 grant. For the full set of guides, visit sassasrdstatuscheckdates.co.za/sassa-account/.

🏦 How to Change Your SASSA Banking Details

If you want your SRD R370 paid into a different account, you can update your banking details at srd.sassa.gov.za.

  1. Go to srd.sassa.gov.za and click "Update Banking Details"
  2. Enter your 13-digit South African ID number
  3. Enter the OTP sent to your registered phone number
  4. Select "Update Banking Details" from the menu
  5. Enter your new bank name, account number, and account type
  6. Confirm and submit — your status will show "Bank Details Pending"
  7. Wait 3–7 working days for SASSA to verify the new account
⚠️ The bank account must be in your own name. SASSA will not pay into someone else's account. If the account is not verified, your payment will be delayed.

📱 How to Change Your SASSA Phone Number

Your registered phone number is used to receive OTPs for all portal access. If you have a new number, follow these steps.

  1. Go to srd.sassa.gov.za
  2. Click "Change Phone Number" or "Update Contact Details"
  3. Enter your ID number and current phone number for verification
  4. If you no longer have access to the old number, you will need to visit a SASSA office in person with your ID
  5. SASSA will verify your identity and update the phone number manually
💡 If you cannot receive the OTP on your old number, do not attempt to change it online. Visit your nearest SASSA office and bring your original SA ID.

📋 How to Apply for the SRD R370 Grant

New applicants can apply online at any time. Applications are free. SASSA never charges a fee.

  1. Go to srd.sassa.gov.za and click "Apply"
  2. Enter your 13-digit South African ID number
  3. Enter your active South African mobile number
  4. Enter the OTP sent to your phone
  5. Provide your banking details (account must be in your name)
  6. Consent to SASSA verifying your income via SARS, UIF, and Home Affairs
  7. Submit and note your reference number
💡 SASSA processes applications within 30 days. Check your status monthly at srd.sassa.gov.za. See our status check guide for all checking methods.

To qualify you must:

  • Be a South African citizen, permanent resident, or refugee
  • Be between 18 and 59 years old
  • Have no income or income below R624/month
  • Not be receiving another SASSA social grant
  • Not be receiving UIF or NSFAS

🔄 How to Reapply for the SRD Grant

If your status shows "Reapplication Required" or your grant has lapsed, you need to submit a new application.

  1. Go to srd.sassa.gov.za — your previous application cannot be reactivated
  2. Click "Apply Now" and complete the form as if applying for the first time
  3. Make sure your phone number and banking details are current before applying
  4. Submit and wait for SASSA to process — this can take up to 30 days
⚠️ Reapplication periods are announced by SASSA. Check SASSA news for the latest announcements on when reapplications open.

🪪 How to Fix Identity Verification Failed

If your status shows "Identity Verification Failed", it means SASSA could not match your details with Home Affairs records.

  1. Go to srd.sassa.gov.za and look for the "Identity Verification" option
  2. Check that your ID number matches your green ID book or Smart Card exactly
  3. If your details are correct but verification still fails, visit your nearest Department of Home Affairs to update your records
  4. After Home Affairs updates their records, try the online verification again after 5–7 working days
