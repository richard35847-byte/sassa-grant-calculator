Contact SASSA 2026

All official ways to reach SASSA — phone, WhatsApp, email, USSD, and in-person offices.

There are several ways to contact SASSA for help with your SRD R370 grant, status queries, banking detail updates, and appeals. Full information is also available at sassasrdstatuscheckdates.co.za/sassa-contact/.

📞

SASSA Toll-Free Helpline

0800 60 10 11

Free to call from any South African landline or mobile phone. Available Monday to Friday, 08:00 – 16:00. Best for general enquiries, status queries, and reporting fraud.

📞 Call Now
💬

SASSA WhatsApp

082 046 8553

Send "Hi" to start the self-service chat. Check your SRD status, get payment information, and update your banking details. Available 24/7 — no data charges for WhatsApp on most networks.

💬 Open WhatsApp
📱

USSD Code — No Data Needed

*120*3210#

Works on any phone — smartphone or basic feature phone — with no internet connection required. Dial and follow the menu to check your SRD status and payment details.

USSD Status Check Guide →
🤖

GovChat Chatbot

0600 123 456

Send a message or call to use the GovChat service for SASSA-related queries. Available on WhatsApp and as a web chat at govchat.org. Handles SRD status, payment queries, and complaints.

📧

SASSA Email

grantsenquiries@sassa.gov.za

For written queries and complaints. Response times can be 5–10 working days. Include your ID number and the nature of your query in the subject line.

SASSA Regional Offices

For complex queries, disability grant applications, or identity-related issues, you may need to visit a SASSA office in person. Offices are open Monday to Friday, 07:30 – 16:00.

⚠️ Scam Warning: SASSA will never ask you to pay a fee to process your grant, update your banking details, or check your status. All official SASSA services are free. If someone asks for money to help you with SASSA, it is a scam. Report suspicious activity to 0800 60 10 11.
