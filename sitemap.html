2

Go to Google Search Console at search.google.com/search-console and add your property: https://richard35847-byte.github.io/sassa-grant-calculator/

3

In the left menu, click Sitemaps → paste sitemap.xml in the field → click Submit.

4

Google will crawl and index your pages, usually within a few days. Check back for indexing status and any errors.

5

Also add this line to your robots.txt file in the repo root:
