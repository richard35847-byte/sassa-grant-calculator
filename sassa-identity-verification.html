SASSA Identity Verification Failed

How to understand this status and fix it — step-by-step guide for 2026.

⚠️ What does "Identity Verification Failed" mean?

When SASSA checks your application, it cross-references your ID number and personal details with the Department of Home Affairs. "Identity Verification Failed" means the details you submitted do not match what Home Affairs has on record. No payment will be made until this is resolved.

Common Causes of Identity Verification Failure

ID Number Mismatch

The ID number entered during application doesn't match Home Affairs records.

Name Spelling Difference

Name on application differs from Home Affairs (e.g., different spelling or middle names).

Outdated Home Affairs Records

Your Home Affairs record has not been updated after a name or status change.

Temporary ID / Refugee Permit

Temporary documents may not link correctly to the verification system.

Duplicate ID Number

Rarely, two people share an ID number due to a Home Affairs error — requires in-person resolution.

System Delays

Home Affairs database delays can cause temporary verification failures that resolve on their own.

🖥️ How to Fix Identity Verification Online

  1. Go to srd.sassa.gov.za
  2. Look for the "Identity Verification" option in the menu
  3. Enter your 13-digit ID number exactly as printed on your ID document
  4. Enter your registered phone number and the OTP sent to it
  5. Complete the verification form — SASSA will recheck your details against Home Affairs
  6. If successful, your status will update within 5–10 working days
💡 Double-check your ID number digit by digit before submitting. A single wrong digit will cause the verification to fail again.

🏢 How to Fix Identity Verification at a SASSA Office

If the online fix doesn't work, you need to visit a SASSA office or Department of Home Affairs in person.

  1. Locate your nearest SASSA office — see Contact SASSA for office addresses
  2. Bring your original South African ID book or Smart Card (no photocopies)
  3. A SASSA official will manually verify your identity and update your record
  4. If the issue is at Home Affairs, they will refer you to the nearest Department of Home Affairs office
  5. After Home Affairs updates their records, your SASSA verification should resolve within 5–7 working days
💡 Visit offices early in the morning to avoid long queues. Bring a copy of all documents as backup.

✅ After Fixing Identity Verification

Once your identity is verified:

  • Your SRD status will update from "Identity Verification Failed" to "Pending" or "Approved"
  • This can take 5–14 working days after the fix
  • Check your status regularly at srd.sassa.gov.za
  • If you are still declined after verification is fixed, you have the right to appeal
