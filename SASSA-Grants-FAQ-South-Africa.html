Disability Grant

How much is the disability grant in South Africa in 2025/2026? ▾

R2,400 /month (Apr 2026)

The SASSA Disability Grant is R2,400 per month from 1 April 2026, up from R2,315. The previous April 2025 amount was R2,310. The grant amount increases twice a year — in April and October — in line with the national budget.

The grant works on a sliding scale: the less private income you earn, the higher the grant amount you receive. Someone with zero income receives the full R2,400, while those with some earnings receive a reduced amount.

Next increase: October 2026. Check sassa.gov.za for the updated figure.
Who qualifies for the SASSA Disability Grant? ▾

To qualify for the Disability Grant you must:

  • Be a South African citizen, permanent resident, or refugee with valid documentation
  • Be aged between 18 and 59 years
  • Have a physical or mental disability that prevents you from working for 6 months or more
  • Pass the means test: earn under R96,840/year if single, or the relevant threshold if married
  • Have assets under R1,372,800 if single
  • Not be a patient in a state institution
  • Not be receiving any other social grant

There are two types:

  • Temporary Disability Grant — for disabilities lasting 6 to 12 months (e.g. TB, recovering from surgery, HIV-related illness)
  • Permanent Disability Grant — for disabilities expected to last longer than 1 year
How do I apply for the disability grant in South Africa? ▾

The disability grant application is a two-step process involving a medical assessment and an in-person application at a SASSA office.

1

Get a medical assessment by a SASSA-appointed doctor (free of charge at a government clinic or hospital). The doctor determines whether you qualify for a temporary or permanent grant.

2

Visit your nearest SASSA office with: your South African ID or valid permit, the completed medical assessment report, proof of income and bank statements, proof of residence, and if married, your spouse's ID and proof of income.

3

Wait for an outcome — SASSA will contact you with a decision. If approved, payment starts from the date SASSA accepts the application.

SASSA services are 100% free. Never pay anyone to help you apply.

HIV Grant

How much is the HIV grant in South Africa? ▾

R2,400 /month (Apr 2026)

There is no separate "HIV grant" in South Africa. SASSA does not offer a standalone grant for HIV/AIDS. Instead, people living with HIV who are unable to work can apply for the SASSA Disability Grant, which pays R2,400 per month from April 2026.

HIV/AIDS is recognised as a qualifying disability when the condition prevents the applicant from supporting themselves financially. A SASSA-appointed doctor must confirm this through a medical assessment. The grant is means-tested.

How to apply for an HIV grant (disability grant) in South Africa ▾

Because the HIV grant is processed as a disability grant, follow these steps:

1

Go to a government hospital or clinic and get a medical assessment. Bring proof of your HIV diagnosis, including a recent CD4 count if available. The assessment is done by a doctor appointed by SASSA and is valid for 3 months.

2

Gather your documents: South African ID or valid refugee/asylum permit, completed medical assessment form, proof of income and bank statements, proof of residence (utility bill or letter from a traditional leader), and if married, your spouse's documents.

3

Apply at your nearest SASSA office. An appointment system is in place — call 0800 60 10 11 (toll-free) to book ahead and avoid long queues.

4

Track your status online at sassa.gov.za or via WhatsApp on 082 046 8553.

How to apply for an HIV grant online in South Africa ▾

Unlike the SRD/R350 grant, the Disability Grant (including HIV-related applications) cannot be completed entirely online — you must attend a SASSA office in person for the medical assessment and document verification.

However, you can:

  • Check your application status online at sassa.gov.za
  • Contact SASSA via WhatsApp: 082 046 8553
  • Call the toll-free helpline: 0800 60 10 11
  • Send an SMS: STATUS [ID number] to 32555

If your application is declined, you have 90 days to submit a written appeal to the Ministry of Social Development. Include any additional medical reports that support your case.

TB (Tuberculosis) Grant

How much is the TB grant in South Africa? ▾

Up to R2,400 /month (Apr 2026)

There is no separate TB grant. South Africans diagnosed with tuberculosis who are unable to work can apply for the SASSA Temporary Disability Grant. TB patients typically receive this grant for 6 to 12 months while they are undergoing treatment, after which their condition is re-evaluated.

The grant amount is the same as the standard disability grant — up to R2,400 per month from April 2026 — paid on a sliding scale based on income. You must pass the means test and not be residing in a state treatment facility.

Good to know: South Africa offers free TB testing and treatment at all public healthcare facilities. SASSA encourages people to seek medical help early so they can access both treatment and financial support promptly.

Child Support Grant

How much is the child support grant in South Africa in 2025/2026? ▾

R580 /month per child (Apr 2026)

The Child Support Grant (CSG) is R580 per month per child from 1 April 2026, increased from R560. An additional top-up of R280 per month applies for double orphans (children who have lost both parents), bringing their total to R860/month.

April 2025 amount was R560, and October 2025 brought it to R580 — the 2026/27 budget maintained R580 from April 2026 with a further small increase announced for later in 2026.

Who qualifies for the child support grant? ▾

The caregiver (parent, grandparent, aunt, uncle, or other primary caregiver) must:

  • Be a South African citizen, permanent resident, refugee, or asylum seeker
  • Be the primary caregiver of a child under 18 who lives with them
  • Pass the means test:
    • Single caregivers: earn under R61,200 per year (~R5,100/month)
    • Married couples: combined income under R122,400 per year

The child must be a South African citizen or permanent resident under the age of 18. A caregiver can apply for multiple children in their care.

Evidence SASSA accepts includes a child's birth certificate, school enrolment letter, clinic card, or an affidavit from a social worker, councillor, or school principal confirming the relationship.

Foster Care Grant

How much is the foster care grant in South Africa? ▾

R1,290 /month (Apr 2026)

The Foster Child Grant is R1,290 per month from 1 April 2026, rising further to R1,300 per month from 1 October 2026. In April 2025 it was R1,250; it increased to R1,290 with the 2026/27 budget.

The Foster Child Grant is not means-tested — unlike most other SASSA grants, your income does not affect whether you receive it or how much you get. However, the foster placement must be confirmed by a Children's Court order.

What is the foster care grant in South Africa and who qualifies? ▾

The Foster Child Grant is a monthly payment to people who are formally fostering a child — that is, caring for a child who has been placed with them by a Children's Court order.

To qualify:

  • You must be a South African citizen or permanent resident
  • You must have a valid Children's Court order naming you as the foster carer
  • The child must be under 18 years old and live with you in South Africa
  • No means test applies — income does not matter

If the foster child also has a severe disability, the foster parent can apply for the Care Dependency Grant in addition to the Foster Child Grant.

Important: You must inform SASSA of any changes to the foster parent's or foster child's circumstances. The grant ends if the court order expires or the child leaves your care.

Care Dependency Grant

What is the care dependency grant in South Africa and how much is it? ▾

R2,400 /month (Apr 2026)

The Care Dependency Grant (CDG) is R2,400 per month from 1 April 2026 (up from R2,315). It is paid to the parent, primary caregiver, or foster parent of a child under 18 who has a severe physical or mental disability that requires full-time home care.

This grant covers the costs of medicine, therapy, assistive devices, and the additional time and support required to care for a child who cannot live independently.

  • The child must live at home — not in a state institution
  • The child's disability must be assessed by a SASSA-appointed doctor
  • The caregiver must pass the means test
  • The caregiver must be a South African citizen or permanent resident

SRD / R350 / R370 Unemployment Grant

How much is the unemployment grant (R350/R370 SRD) in South Africa? ▾

R370 /month (extended to Mar 2027)

The Social Relief of Distress (SRD) grant — still commonly called the "R350 grant" after its original amount — pays R370 per month. It has been extended until at least 31 March 2027. The SRD grant amount has not changed since it was raised from R350 to R370.

It targets unemployed South Africans who have no other source of income and do not receive any other social grant or UIF payment.

How to apply for the R350/R370 SRD grant in South Africa ▾

The SRD grant is the only SASSA grant that can be applied for entirely online — no office visit required.

1

Go to srd.sassa.gov.za or send a WhatsApp message to 082 046 8553

2

Enter your South African ID number and your registered cellphone number. Confirm with the OTP sent via SMS.

3

Complete the application form with your personal details, employment status, and bank details (optional but speeds up payment).

4

Submit and wait for an SMS within 2–3 weeks. SASSA re-assesses eligibility every month — you do not need to re-apply each month, but your details must remain accurate.

You can also apply via the SASSA chatbot on the official website or by calling 0800 60 10 11.

Eligibility checklist: Aged 18–60 · South African citizen, permanent resident, refugee, or asylum seeker · Earn less than R624/month · Not receiving UIF, NSFAS, or any other SASSA grant · Not residing in a state institution
Why was my SRD R370 application declined? ▾

Common reasons for SRD grant declines include:

  • Income exceeds R624 per month (SASSA checks SARS tax records, UIF records, and bank account deposits)
  • You are already receiving another SASSA grant or UIF benefit
  • Mismatched ID number or cellphone number
  • Incorrect banking details
  • You are registered as receiving NSFAS funding

If declined, you can appeal within 90 days through srd.sassa.gov.za. SASSA must provide a written reason for the decline.

More SASSA Questions & Answers

How do I apply for the SASSA R350 grant step by step?▾

The SASSA R350 grant — now officially the Social Relief of Distress (SRD) grant paying R370/month — can be applied for entirely online. To qualify you must be aged 18–59, be unemployed, not receive UIF or any other social grant, and have insufficient financial means.

1

Go to srd.sassa.gov.za — the official SASSA SRD portal.

2

Enter your South African ID number and your registered cellphone number.

3

Verify your phone number using the OTP sent via SMS.

4

Accept the declaration and consent form, then complete your personal details.

5

Provide your banking information or select an alternative payment method (e.g. retail cash payment).

6

Submit the application. SASSA will verify your information through government databases (SARS, UIF, NSFAS) and notify you by SMS within 2–3 weeks.

Application is free. You only need to apply once — SASSA re-assesses eligibility each month automatically.
How do I check my SASSA R350 / SRD status online?▾
1

Visit srd.sassa.gov.za and go to the Status Check page.

2

Enter your South African ID number.

3

Enter the phone number used during registration.

4

Click "Check Status" to view your result.

Your status will show one of four outcomes:

  • Approved — grant approved; a payment date will be assigned.
  • Pending — your application is still under review.
  • Declined — SASSA found you don't meet one or more requirements for that month. You can appeal.
  • Referred — additional identity or income verification is being conducted.

Checking your status regularly helps you catch declines early and lodge an appeal within the 90-day window.

How do I change my phone number on SASSA R350 / SRD?▾

If you lost your phone, changed networks, or registered the wrong number, you can update your SASSA SRD contact number online:

1

Visit srd.sassa.gov.za and select "Update Contact Information."

2

Enter your ID number and verify your identity.

3

Enter your new cellphone number and complete OTP verification on the new number.

4

Submit the request. Future SMS notifications and payment alerts will be sent to the new number.

Important: The new number must be registered in your name and must be active. An incorrect or inactive number can delay grant processing.
How do I change my banking details for SASSA grants?▾

You can update your SASSA banking details when you change banks, close an old account, or need to correct an error. The process applies to both the SRD grant and permanent grants.

1

Visit the official SASSA website and select "Banking Details Update."

2

Enter your South African ID number.

3

SASSA will send a secure verification link to your registered cellphone via SMS.

4

Open the secure link and enter your new banking information (bank name, branch code, account number).

5

Submit the changes. Processing may take several business days.

  • The bank account must be in your own name — SASSA will not pay into someone else's account.
  • Your personal details must match your bank records exactly.
  • Incorrect details delay payments.
How do I check my SASSA balance?▾

There are several ways to check whether your SASSA grant payment has been deposited:

  • USSD code: Dial the balance-check code provided by your payment provider (e.g. Postbank: *120*3210#).
  • ATM: Insert your SASSA card and select "Balance Inquiry" — free at most SASSA-linked ATMs.
  • Retail stores: Participating retailers (Shoprite, Pick n Pay, Boxer) can confirm your balance at the till.
  • Bank app or internet banking: If your grant is paid into a commercial bank account, use your banking app to check the balance instantly.
Checking your balance before going to the ATM saves time and avoids unnecessary trips — especially on busy grant payment days.
How do I check my SASSA payment date?▾

SASSA releases a monthly payment schedule before each month begins. Payment days are staggered by grant type to reduce queues:

  • Day 1: Older Persons Grant
  • Day 2: Disability Grant
  • Day 3: Children's Grants (Child Support, Foster Care, Care Dependency) and all remaining grants

To check your specific payment date:

  • Use the SASSA SRD status check at srd.sassa.gov.za
  • View the monthly payment calendar on this site
  • Call 0800 60 10 11 (toll-free)
  • Visit your nearest SASSA office
Payment dates occasionally shift due to public holidays or weekends. Always check the official schedule before month-end.
How do I get my SASSA verification link?▾

The SASSA verification link is a secure one-time URL sent to your registered cellphone via SMS. It is used for updating banking details, phone numbers, and other account information.

1

Visit the relevant SASSA online service (e.g. banking details update at sassa.gov.za).

2

Enter your ID number and request verification.

3

SASSA sends a secure link via SMS to your registered number.

4

Open the link and complete the required update.

Security: Only click links sent from SASSA's official number. Never share your OTP with anyone. SASSA will never send a payment link asking for your banking details proactively.
Who qualifies for the SASSA Old Age Grant (state pension)?▾

The Older Persons Grant — commonly called the state pension — pays R2,400/month (under 75) or R2,420/month (75 and older) from April 2026. To qualify:

  • Be 60 years or older
  • Be a South African citizen, permanent resident, or refugee
  • Live in South Africa
  • Pass the means test: earn under R95,400/year if single, or R190,800/year combined if married
  • Have assets under R1,372,800 (single) or R2,745,600 (married)
  • Not be receiving another social grant for yourself
  • Not be maintained in a state institution

Required documents: South African ID, proof of residence, proof of income (bank statements, payslips), and banking details. Apply at your nearest SASSA office.

How do I appeal a declined SASSA SRD grant?▾

If your SRD application was declined, you can appeal the decision. Common reasons for declines include an incorrect income assessment, identity verification issues, banking errors, or administrative mistakes.

1

Visit the SRD Appeals platform at srd.sassa.gov.za.

2

Enter your ID number and verify using your registered cellphone number.

3

Select the declined month you wish to appeal.

4

Submit your appeal. An independent reviewer will assess your case.

You must appeal within 90 days of the decline. If successful, SASSA pays the grant for the affected month retroactively. You will be notified of the outcome by SMS.

For disability grant appeals, submit a written appeal to the Ministry of Social Development within 90 days, including any additional medical reports or supporting documents.

How do I cancel my SASSA R350 / SRD grant?▾

You may want to cancel your SRD grant if your financial situation improves, you find employment, or you begin receiving another grant or UIF benefit.

1

Visit srd.sassa.gov.za and access the cancellation section.

2

Enter your ID number and verify your identity.

3

Confirm the cancellation. Future payments will stop, and your application status will become inactive.

You can reapply later if your circumstances change and you become eligible again. Consider your situation carefully before cancelling.
When is the SASSA payment for December 2025?▾

SASSA releases confirmed payment dates before the start of each month. For December 2025, payments followed the standard staggered schedule:

  • Older Persons Grant — first payment day of December
  • Disability Grant — second payment day
  • Children's Grants & all remaining grants — third payment day

Exact dates can shift when public holidays fall on or near payment days. For future months, check the SASSA payment dates page on this site, the official sassa.gov.za, or call 0800 60 10 11 for the confirmed schedule.

Payment dates are announced monthly. Bookmark the payment dates page to stay updated.
