All SASSA Grants 2026

Amounts, eligibility requirements, and how to apply for every SASSA social grant in South Africa.

SASSA administers several social grants on behalf of the South African government. Each grant targets a specific group. Below is a complete guide to every grant available in 2026. For the most current information, visit sassasrdstatuscheckdates.co.za/sassa-grants/.

SRD R370 Grant (Social Relief of Distress)
R370 / month
Formerly the R350 grant — increased April 2023

The SRD grant supports unemployed South Africans aged 18–59 who have no other income. It is the only SASSA grant you can apply for entirely online.

Who qualifies?
  • SA citizen, permanent resident, or refugee
  • Aged 18–59
  • No income or below R624/month
  • Not receiving another SASSA grant
  • Not receiving UIF or NSFAS
How to apply
  • Online: srd.sassa.gov.za
  • Need: SA ID, phone number, bank account
  • Free to apply — SASSA never charges fees

Older Persons Grant (Old Age Pension)
R2,400 / month
Paid on the 1st–2nd working day of each month

The Older Persons Grant, commonly called the old age pension, is for South Africans aged 60 and above who pass a means test.

Who qualifies?
  • Aged 60 or older
  • SA citizen or permanent resident
  • Income below R86,280/year (single) or R172,560/year (married)
  • Assets below R1,227,600 (single)
How to apply
  • Apply at your nearest SASSA office
  • Bring: ID, proof of residence, bank details, proof of income
  • Processing can take up to 3 months

Disability Grant
R2,400 / month
Requires a medical assessment — temporary or permanent

The Disability Grant supports people aged 18–59 who have a disability that prevents them from working. It can be granted temporarily or permanently depending on the medical assessment.

Who qualifies?
  • Aged 18–59
  • SA citizen or permanent resident
  • Have a disability that prevents work
  • Pass the means test (similar to Older Persons)
  • Medical assessment required
Documents needed
  • SA ID
  • Medical report from a registered doctor
  • Proof of residence and income
  • Bank account details

Child Support Grant
R580 / child per month
For primary caregivers of children under 18

The Child Support Grant is paid to the primary caregiver of a child under 18. It is one of the most widely received grants in South Africa.

Who qualifies?
  • Primary caregiver of a child under 18
  • SA citizen or permanent resident
  • Income below R4,800/month (single) or R9,600/month (married)
  • Child must be SA citizen or permanent resident
How to apply
  • Apply at any SASSA office
  • Bring: ID, child's birth certificate, proof of residence
  • Can apply for multiple children

Foster Child Grant
R1,290 / child per month
For children in foster care — requires court order

The Foster Child Grant supports foster parents caring for a child under an official foster care order from a Children's Court.

Who qualifies?
  • Foster parent with a Children's Court foster order
  • Child must be under 18 (or under 22 if studying)
  • No means test for this grant
Documents needed
  • Foster care court order
  • Child's birth certificate
  • Foster parent's ID and bank details
❤️
Care Dependency Grant
R2,400 / month
For caregivers of children with severe disabilities

Paid to caregivers of children (under 18) who require full-time care and support due to a severe physical or mental disability.

Who qualifies?
  • Caring for a child under 18 with a severe disability
  • The child requires permanent care/support
  • Parent/caregiver must be SA citizen or permanent resident
  • Pass the means test
Documents needed
  • Child's ID / birth certificate
  • Medical assessment by SASSA doctor
  • Caregiver's ID and bank details
  • Proof of residence
