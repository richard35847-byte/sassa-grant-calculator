SASSA SRD Status Meanings 2026

Every possible status result explained in plain language — and exactly what you should do next.

When you check your SRD R370 status at srd.sassa.gov.za, you will see one of 8 possible results. This guide explains each one clearly. For the full status check guide, visit sassasrdstatuscheckdates.co.za/sassa-status-meanings/.

All 8 SASSA SRD Status Results

✅ APPROVED

Approved

Your application for this month has been successful. SASSA has verified your identity and confirmed you meet the eligibility criteria. Payment will be processed and you will receive an SMS when the money is ready to collect or deposited.

What to do:
  • Wait for an SMS from SASSA confirming your payment date
  • Check your bank account or collect at your nominated point
  • SRD R370 payments are made between the 21st–30th of each month
  • See payment dates for the current month's schedule
🕐 PENDING / PENDING VERIFICATION

Pending

Your application has been received and is still being processed. SASSA cross-checks your details with Home Affairs, SARS, UIF, and other government databases before approving any payment. This can take from a few days to several weeks.

What to do:
  • Do nothing — this is normal. Check back after the 15th of the month.
  • Make sure your registered phone number is still active
  • Pending does NOT mean declined — you still have a chance of approval
  • See our status check guide for all ways to monitor your application
❌ DECLINED

Declined

Your application was not approved for this month. SASSA found a reason you do not qualify — common causes include having another source of income, receiving UIF, being registered for NSFAS, or an income above R624/month.

Common decline reasons:
  • Alternative income detected via SARS records
  • Registered for UIF or NSFAS
  • Receiving another SASSA social grant
  • Income threshold exceeded (above R624/month)
  • Registered in a government institution
Appeal a Declined Status →

You have 90 days from each declined decision to submit an appeal. See the full SASSA SRD appeal guide.

⚠️ IDENTITY VERIFICATION FAILED

Identity Verification Failed

SASSA could not verify your identity with the Department of Home Affairs. This usually means there is a mismatch between your ID number and your personal details on record, or your Home Affairs record has an error.

What to do:
  • Go to srd.sassa.gov.za and use the "Identity Verification" link
  • Check that your ID number and name are entered exactly as on your green ID book or Smart Card
  • If the problem persists, visit your nearest SASSA office or Department of Home Affairs
  • See the full guide: Identity Verification Guide
🔄 REAPPLICATION REQUIRED

Reapplication Required

Your original application has lapsed or a new application period has opened. SASSA requires you to submit a fresh application to continue receiving the grant.

What to do:
  • Go to srd.sassa.gov.za and click "Apply Now"
  • Fill in all your details again — this is a completely new application
  • Make sure your bank account and phone number are current
  • See Account Help for a step-by-step reapplication guide
🏦 BANK DETAILS PENDING

Bank Details Pending

You have recently updated your banking information and SASSA is verifying the new account. This process usually takes 3–7 working days. No payment will be made until the account is confirmed.

What to do:
  • Wait 3–7 working days for the account to be verified
  • Make sure the bank account is in your own name
  • Do not change your banking details again during this period
  • See the banking details update guide for more help
📋 NOT CAPTURED / APPLICATION NOT FOUND

Not Captured / Application Not Found

SASSA cannot find an application linked to your ID number. This may mean your application was not successfully submitted, or you searched with the wrong ID number or phone number.

What to do:
  • Double-check you entered the correct 13-digit ID number
  • Make sure you are using the same phone number you applied with
  • If you have not applied yet, go to srd.sassa.gov.za to apply
  • Visit status check guide for all check methods
🚫 CANCELLED

Cancelled

Your grant has been cancelled. This happens if SASSA later finds you no longer meet the eligibility requirements, or if you requested cancellation yourself.

What to do:
  • Contact SASSA to understand the reason for cancellation
  • If you believe the cancellation is an error, submit an appeal
  • Call SASSA toll-free: 0800 60 10 11
  • See Contact SASSA for all contact options
