SASSA SRD FAQ 2026

The 25 most frequently asked questions about the SASSA SRD R370 grant — answered clearly and simply.

Can't find what you're looking for here? Visit the full guide at sassasrdstatuscheckdates.co.za/faq/.

Status Check Questions

How do I check my SASSA SRD status?

There are 4 ways to check your SRD R370 status:

  • Online: Go to srd.sassa.gov.za, enter your ID and phone number
  • WhatsApp: Message 082 046 8553 and send "Hi"
  • USSD: Dial *120*3210# — no data needed
  • Call: 0800 60 10 11 (free, Mon–Fri 08:00–16:00)

See the full SASSA status check guide for step-by-step instructions.

What does SASSA Pending status mean?

Pending means SASSA has received your application but has not yet finished processing it for the current month. SASSA verifies your details with Home Affairs, SARS, and UIF — this can take until around the 15th–20th of the month.

Pending does not mean declined. Check again after the 15th. See all status meanings explained.

Why is my status still Pending after 3 weeks?

Sometimes verification takes longer due to high volumes or database delays. If your status is still Pending after the 20th of the month, try:

  • Calling 0800 60 10 11 to enquire
  • WhatsApping 082 046 8553
  • Visiting a SASSA office

You can also contact SASSA for more information.

How often does SASSA update statuses?

SASSA updates SRD statuses once per month, usually between the 10th and 20th. After that the status is set for that month. New statuses are set each month, so a Declined status one month does not automatically mean Declined next month.

Payment Questions

When does SASSA pay SRD R370 each month?

The SRD R370 is paid in batches between the 21st and 30th of each month. Exact dates vary — SASSA releases the schedule monthly. See the payment dates page for the current month's schedule.

I was Approved but I haven't received payment. What do I do?

If your status shows Approved but you have not received payment:

  • Check that your bank account details are correct at srd.sassa.gov.za
  • Wait until the end of the payment window (around the 30th)
  • Check your status for "Bank Details Pending" or errors
  • Call 0800 60 10 11 if payment has not arrived by the 1st of the following month

See how to update banking details if your account may be incorrect.

What is the SASSA SRD payment amount in 2026?

The SRD grant is currently R370 per month. It was increased from R350 to R370 in April 2023. See the full list of SASSA grant amounts 2026.

Where can I collect my SASSA SRD payment?

Payment is made to the bank account you registered with SASSA. If you registered a bank account, the money is deposited directly. If you need to update your payment method, visit banking details update guide.

SASSA stopped paying at Post Office and retail outlets for the SRD grant — direct bank payment is the primary method.

Declined & Appeal Questions

Why was my SASSA SRD application declined?

Common reasons for a Declined status include:

  • Income detected via SARS (even informal income)
  • Receiving UIF payments
  • Registered for NSFAS
  • Receiving another SASSA social grant
  • Income above R624/month
  • Not meeting the age requirement (18–59)

If you believe the decline is incorrect, you can appeal. See the SRD appeal guide.

How do I appeal a declined SASSA SRD application?

You have 90 days from the declined decision to appeal. Go to srd.sassa.gov.za, navigate to the Appeals section, select the declined month, and submit your reason. Your appeal is reviewed by the Independent Tribunal for Social Assistance Appeals (ITSAA).

See the full appeal step-by-step guide.

How long does a SASSA appeal take?

Appeals are typically processed within 60–90 days. You will receive an SMS once a decision is made. Check the status of your appeal at srd.sassa.gov.za under the Appeals section.

Eligibility Questions

Who qualifies for the SASSA SRD R370 grant?

To qualify you must:

  • Be a South African citizen, permanent resident, or refugee
  • Be between 18 and 59 years old
  • Have no income, or income below R624/month
  • Not be receiving any other SASSA grant
  • Not be receiving UIF or NSFAS
  • Not be in a government-funded institution

Use the SASSA Grant Eligibility Calculator to check your eligibility instantly.

Is the R350 grant still active in 2026?

The R350 grant was renamed and increased to R370 in April 2023. It is the same grant — same portal, same eligibility, same WhatsApp number. Only the amount changed. If you see "R350" in old messages, that refers to your current SRD R370 account.

Can I get both the SRD grant and the child support grant?

No. SASSA rules state that a person cannot receive the SRD grant and another SASSA social grant at the same time. The Child Support Grant is paid to the caregiver of the child. If you are the caregiver receiving the Child Support Grant, you are not eligible for the SRD grant.

Account & Technical Questions

How do I update my SASSA banking details?

Log in to srd.sassa.gov.za using your ID and OTP. Select "Update Banking Details" and enter your new account details. The account must be in your own name. Verification takes 3–7 working days. See the full banking details guide.

How do I change my SASSA registered phone number?

You can attempt to change your phone number at srd.sassa.gov.za. If you no longer have access to the old number, you must visit a SASSA office in person with your original SA ID. See the phone number change guide.

What does "Identity Verification Failed" mean?

SASSA could not match your details with Home Affairs records. This usually means a mismatch in your ID number or name. Try the identity verification option at srd.sassa.gov.za, or visit a SASSA/Home Affairs office. See the full identity verification guide.

Can I apply for SASSA without a bank account?

You need a bank account in your own name to receive the SRD grant. If you do not have an account, you can open a free basic bank account at most South African banks or at the Post Office (Postbank). SASSA does not currently offer cash collection for the SRD grant.
